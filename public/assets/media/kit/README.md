# Cash Pass brand kit

Every mark in this kit is real SVG drawn from the brand graphic kit's motif, the lit card on glass. No mark was generated as a raster. The wordmark is Familjen Grotesk 700 converted to outlines, so the files render without the font installed.

## The motif
A soft pool of cool light on black glass, a card standing on it, and the card's reflection fading into the glass. The card carries a short bar where its ending digits would sit. It reads as the one card the light has found.

## Files
- logo/cashpass-primary.svg: the horizontal lockup for light grounds (mark in oxblood 2E0B0C, wordmark 2E0B0C, pool 2E0B0C at 20 percent).
- logo/cashpass-reversed.svg: the horizontal lockup for dark grounds (card E9F1F8, pool E9F1F8 at 35 percent, wordmark F5EDE8). This is the version in the site nav and in the app header.
- logo/cashpass-stacked.svg and cashpass-stacked-reversed.svg: the mark centred above the wordmark, 200 by 220, for avatars and directory listings.
- logo/cashpass-icon.svg and cashpass-icon-reversed.svg: the mark alone on a 64 by 56 box.
- logo/cashpass-primary.png (2048 wide, transparent), cashpass-reversed.png (2048 wide, transparent), cashpass-icon-512.png (transparent), favicon.svg, favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), apple-touch-icon.png (180).
- banners/linkedin-cover-1128x191.png, x-header-1500x500.png, crunchbase-1280x720.png, og-card-1200x630.png.
- kit/: the graphic kit (motif.svg with ids for the draw in, seal.svg, three dividers, icons.svg as a symbol sprite with icons.json, grain.svg).

No green, blue, orange, yellow, pink or violet anywhere. The field is the only red and red is never an accent.

## Type
Familjen Grotesk (weights 400 to 700) for everything; headings at 600 to 700 with letter spacing minus 0.015em. Instrument Serif regular for the answer line only ("Tap Grocery 6%"). Money figures in Familjen 600 with tabular numerals and the $ and % signs at 85 percent size, raised 8 percent.

## Which version on which ground
The primary (dark) lockup goes on white and on the ice white accent. The reversed (light) lockup goes on the oxblood field, on black and on any dark photograph. Never place the reversed lockup on a light ground or the primary on the field.

## Clear space
Keep one card width (40 units of the 64 unit mark, or 62 percent of the mark's width) clear on every side of any lockup. Never rotate, skew, outline, add a drop shadow to or recolour the mark outside the two colourways.

## Minimum size
Horizontal lockup 96px wide. Icon 24px. Below 32px use the favicon version, which has no reflection.

## Motion
The motif draws in with the pool brightening over 0.42s (ease cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.3, 1)), the card's fill rising from 5E1F22 to E9F1F8 over 0.42s and the reflection following 80ms later. Under prefers-reduced-motion it is simply present.
